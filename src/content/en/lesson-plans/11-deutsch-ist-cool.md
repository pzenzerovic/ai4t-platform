## Objective

Students pass through a full learning cycle in German at A2 level — written practice, a team task, speaking, and a graded test — using one connected set of materials rather than five unrelated ones.

## Preparation

- Print the worksheet "Deutsch ist cool!" (6 tasks and a mini quiz), one per student.
- Print the escape room sheet with its four stations and prepare a code slip for each team.
- Print the Deutsch Memes sheet — either as a handout or enlarged for display.
- Print and cut the nine speaking cards; one set per group.
- Print the mini test (three parts, 40 points) for the assessment lesson, and prepare the marking key.

## Lesson flow

1. **Lesson 1 · 45 min — practice** — Open with the Deutsch Memes sheet: six everyday teenage situations, each with a short German line of up to five words, closing with the message *Fehler sind erlaubt. Übung macht den Meister.* The point of the opening is to lower the fear of making mistakes. The rest of the lesson is the worksheet: six tasks of different types plus a mini quiz.
2. **Lesson 2 · 45 min — team task and speaking** — Escape Room *Mission Deutsch*: four stations, each a different kind of language exercise (prepositions, sentence building, error correction, spotting the odd one out), each yielding one letter of a four-letter Geheimcode. The remaining time is for the speaking cards: nine cards, each with one question to be answered in five or six sentences (weekend, hobbies, free time, rooms, friendship, mobile phone, travel, summer, the perfect day).
3. **Lesson 3 · 45 min — assessment** — Mini test in three parts, 40 points in total: *Wortschatz* (10 points) — five translations from Croatian into German plus three multiple-choice items; *Grammatik* (15 points) — five sentences requiring the correct verb form and five with prepositions; *Schreiben* (15 points) — eight to ten sentences about oneself, guided by set questions.

## Learning outcomes

- Students can use A2-level vocabulary from the covered topics in written tasks.
- Students can put a German sentence together correctly and choose the right preposition.
- Students can find and correct an error in a given German sentence.
- Students can speak for five to six sentences on a familiar topic without preparation.
- Students can complete a written task of eight to ten sentences about themselves following set questions.

## Notes

- The pack came out of a single generative pass, which is what makes the parts fit together: the same vocabulary and the same level run through practice, game, speaking and test.
- The memes are not decoration. They are there so that a student who is afraid of mistakes starts the lesson laughing rather than bracing.
- The types of station in the escape room, the themes of the speaking cards and the structure of the test are specified in the prompts published alongside the pack.
- Check the German before printing — grammatical gender, prepositions and idiomatic phrasing are exactly where a generated text quietly goes wrong.
- The material was created with ChatGPT and is published for reuse. Students do not use an AI tool in these lessons.
