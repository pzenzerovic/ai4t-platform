## Objective

Students combine movement and knowledge about sport, hydration, fair play and healthy eating to solve six station tasks and assemble the secret sports code.

## Preparation

- Print the main poster (A3 if possible) and display it where all teams can see it.
- Print the sheet of station cards, cut it into six cards and place one card at each station around the gym.
- Prepare a pencil and an answer slip for each team; teams of 4–6 students.
- Station 6 needs free floor space for squats, jumps and a plank — check the surface and the spacing between teams.
- Decide the rotation order in advance so that no two teams start at the same station.

## Lesson flow

1. **Mission briefing** · 5 min — The teacher presents the poster: solve the tasks, collect the code, escape from the gym. Teams are formed, the rotation is explained and the rule is set that a station is left only once its answer is written down.
2. **Station circuit** · 30 min, about 5 minutes per station — Teams rotate through six stations: **1 Guess the sport** (match four descriptions to basketball, football, volleyball and tennis); **2 Hydration** (circle the true statements: drink water during sport — true; energy drinks are the best choice for children — false; the body loses water through sweating — true; water helps concentration — true); **3 Fair play** (choose the correct meaning of fair play out of three options); **4 Olympic rings** (how many rings are there: 4, 5 or 6); **5 Healthy choice** (circle the healthier option in four pairs); **6 Mini fitness challenge** (10 squats, 5 jumps, 10 seconds of plank — completing it earns the final digit of the code).
3. **The secret sports code** · 5 min — Teams enter the six results in order into the code field on the poster and check the code with the teacher.
4. **Bonus mission and closing** · 5 min — Each team invents a team name, a slogan and a victory move and presents it. Closing message: get moving, have fun, be the best version of yourself.

## Learning outcomes

- Students can recognise sports from a description of their basic rules and equipment.
- Students can distinguish true from false statements about hydration during sport.
- Students can explain what fair play means and give an example of it.
- Students can choose the healthier option in a pair of everyday foods or drinks and justify the choice.
- Students can complete a short exercise set (squats, jumps, plank) with correct technique and as part of a team.

## Notes

- The kit works in a small gym, a corridor or outdoors — the stations need no equipment beyond the printed cards.
- Answers at stations 2, 4 and 5 are factual and should be checked against the school's own health-education material before printing; the material was generated with an AI tool and its factual claims are the teacher's responsibility.
- Teams that finish a station early can be sent to repeat the fitness challenge rather than waiting, which keeps the circuit moving.
- The material was created with ChatGPT and is published for reuse. Students do not use an AI tool in this lesson.
